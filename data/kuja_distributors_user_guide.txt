Stock Match


Every morning, a system pop-up prompts distributors to perform a physical stock count, verify it against system records, and resolve any discrepancies.

In cases where physical stock matches with system stock, the distributor clicks on "stock Matches"

If there are discrepancies, the distributor adjusts system stock to reflect the physical stock counted.
