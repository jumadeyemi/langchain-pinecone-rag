Outlet Home page

To place order for your customer

I. Select the product you are buying

II. Input the quantity you want to buy.

III. Click on "Confirm order"

To place order for your customer

View Order Summary

On the Order Summary, you can delete products you no longer want, increase or reduce the quantity, and click "Confirm" to proceed.

View Order Summary

Empties to return

I. Once you confirm your order, you will input the empties you need to return

Select how you want to receive orders

I. Select how you would like your customer to receive the order, either through store delivery or pickup.

II. Click on "Yes, Place Order" to confirm your order.

III. Please note that the system will indicate that the order was placed on behalf of the customer.
